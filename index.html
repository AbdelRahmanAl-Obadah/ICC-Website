Innovation & Computing Club is a student-run organization at the German Jordanian University, focused on making academic life easier through technology.

We build the tools we wish existed as students — clear curriculum maps, organized requirements and a searchable home for everything computing students need to plan their degree. This platform is the club's flagship project, developed in phases and maintained by student contributors.

A student-built academic resources platform for GJU's computing community.
